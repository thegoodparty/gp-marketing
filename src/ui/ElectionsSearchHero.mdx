# Elections Search Hero

<Description of={ElectionsSearchHeroStories.Default} />

## Overview

The hero at the top of the Elections page. A left-aligned headline and body copy sit above a city or county search, and a
carousel of photos with a floating quote card sits beside them. On phones the columns stack and the quote card hangs
below the photo.

The search is `ElectionsNearYouSearch`, the same form the Elections Near You Block renders: Google Places suggestions,
the resolve-place lookup, the analytics events and the navigation are shared, so the two blocks cannot drift apart.

## Variants

### Default

<Canvas of={ElectionsSearchHeroStories.Default} />

Cream background, three slides. The dots and arrows only appear with two or more slides.

### Midnight

<Canvas of={ElectionsSearchHeroStories.Midnight} />

The quote card stays yellow with black text; everything else inverts.

### Single slide

<Canvas of={ElectionsSearchHeroStories.SingleSlide} />

No dots or arrows.

### Photo only

<Canvas of={ElectionsSearchHeroStories.PhotoOnly} />

A slide with neither a quote nor an author renders the photo alone.

### No slides

<Canvas of={ElectionsSearchHeroStories.NoSlides} />

The carousel column is left out and the hero is the text and search alone. This is what the live Elections page renders
until an editor adds slides.

## Props

<Controls of={ElectionsSearchHeroStories.Default} />

#### `slides` (optional)

One entry per carousel slide: `image` (required, shown as a square), `quote` and `author` (`name`, `meta`, `image`).
The author's image is the small avatar on the quote card; without one the card shows the name and title alone.

#### `buttonLabel` (optional)

Label on the search button. Defaults to "Search".

#### `backgroundColor` (optional)

`'cream'` or `'midnight'`. Defaults to `'midnight'` because the live document was saved before the redesign and has
that value.
