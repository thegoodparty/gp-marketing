# Profile Hero

<Description of={ProfileHeroStories.DefaultStory} />

## Overview

The ProfileHero component is the header of a person's profile: persona tag, name, office line, a short intro paragraph about the voter guide, and a pledge callout that states whether the person took the GoodParty.org Pledge, with a "Read the full pledge" link that opens the pledge pop-up. The portrait straddles the dark band and hangs into the content below.

The intro and the callout were added after design feedback on the Voter Guide frames (Emily, 2026-10-06); before that the hero carried a one-line pledge status under the office. The legacy `/candidate` pages still render the older framing: no intro, no callout, and the "Empowered by GoodParty.org" line.

## Usage

Use this component when:
- Displaying the hero section on `/people` profile pages (through `ProfileHeroSection`, which fills the intro from Studio)
- Showing the person's pledge status and GoodParty.org branding for claimed profiles

### Prop Details

#### `candidateName` (required)

The candidate's full name displayed as the main heading.

**Type:** `string`

#### `office` (required)

The office or position the candidate is running for or holds.

**Type:** `string`

#### `profileImage` (optional)

The candidate's profile image as a Sanity image object. Used on CMS-driven pages.

**Type:** `SanityImage`

#### `profileImageUrl` (optional)

The candidate's profile image as a hosted URL. Used on dynamic candidate profile pages from the Elections API.

**Type:** `string`

#### `isEmpowered` (optional)

When true, shows the GoodParty logo badge on the avatar and the "Empowered by GoodParty.org" attribution. Set from claimed campaign lookup on candidate profile pages. This is the legacy fallback, used only where `attribution` is not passed — today that is `/candidate/[...slug]`.

**Type:** `boolean`

**Default:** `false`

#### `attribution` (optional)

What the hero says about the person. Overrides `isEmpowered` when passed.

The three pledge values render the **pledge callout**: a bordered box under the intro with one sentence and the "Read the full pledge" pop-up link. The `/people` person profiles pass one of them; `personSectionOverrides.pledgeAttribution` decides which from the pledge flag and party evidence, and `pledgeAttributionCopy.test.tsx` pins the wording:

- `pledged` → "This candidate took the GoodParty.org Pledge, promising to serve people first, independent of both major parties and big-money interests." (with the heart-and-star mark beside it)
- `notPledged` → "This candidate has not yet taken the GoodParty.org Pledge to serve people first, independent of both major parties and big-money interests."
- `pledgeIneligible` → "This candidate is ineligible for the GoodParty.org Pledge due to partisan affiliation."

`empowered` renders the legacy "Empowered by GoodParty.org" line (the `/candidate` framing); `none` renders nothing.

The sentences live in `~/ui/_lib/attributionCopy` (`pledgeCalloutCopy`), next to the short lines the related-person cards still use. Take the wording from there rather than restating it — the cards went on publishing a retired sentence precisely because they held their own copy of it.

**Type:** `AttributionMode` — `'empowered' | 'pledged' | 'notPledged' | 'pledgeIneligible' | 'none'`

**Default:** derived from `isEmpowered`

#### `pledgeSubject` (optional)

Who the callout sentence is about: `'candidate'` ("This candidate…") or `'elected official'` ("This elected official…"). The frames only draw candidates; the swap for people who hold or held office is marketing's (Emily, 2026-10-06). `/people` sets it from the persona: officeholders and past officeholders are elected officials, everyone running (including someone serving and running) is a candidate.

**Type:** `'candidate' | 'elected official'`

**Default:** `'candidate'`

#### `intro` (optional)

The paragraph between the office line and the callout. Tokens are already resolved by the caller. On `/people`, `ProfileHeroSection` reads it from the block's Text tab in Studio ("Intro for candidates" / "Intro for elected officials"), falling back to the preset in `~/lib/profileHeroDefaults` when the field is empty, and fills `[candidate name]`. Nothing here means no paragraph, which is what the `/candidate` pages render.

**Type:** `string`

#### `showBrandMark` (optional)

Whether the GoodParty.org mark paints on the portrait (and beside the legacy attribution line). Separate from `attribution` because the mark says the page is a GoodParty.org profile while the callout asserts a fact about the person — on `/people` those come from different inputs (claimed vs pledged). The mark inside the pledge callout is not this one: it follows the pledge, because there it illustrates the sentence beside it.

**Type:** `boolean`

**Default:** `attribution === 'empowered'`

#### `backgroundColor` (optional)

Background color variant for the hero section.

**Type:** `'cream' | 'midnight'`

**Default:** `'midnight'`

#### `className` (optional)

Additional CSS classes to apply to the component.

**Type:** `string`

## Layout

The component uses a responsive flex layout:
- **Mobile**: Vertical stack (image, then tag, name, office, intro, callout), the dark band covering the whole hero
- **Desktop**: Portrait on the left, text column on the right; the band's height follows the text column, so a long office line or a wrapped intro makes the band taller rather than spilling onto the cream below
- The portrait is anchored to the bottom of the row and overflows the band by a fixed amount (48px from `md`, 68px from `lg`, per the frames). `ProfileContentBlock`'s sidebar offsets by the same amount to clear it, so the two must move together.

## Avatar and Badge

When a profile image is provided (`profileImageUrl` or `profileImage`):
- Image is displayed in a circular container
- The GoodParty logo badge overlays the avatar only when `showBrandMark` is true (or, for the legacy pages, `isEmpowered`)
- Avatar size is responsive: 160px on mobile, 288px from `md`, 416px from `lg`

## Pledge callout

An 8px-radius box with a hairline border and a faint fill over the band (midnight/200 line, midnight/50 at 10%). The heart-and-star mark sits on the left only when the person took the pledge; on the phone it sits above the sentence. "GoodParty.org Pledge" is set in bold inside the sentence, and "Read the full pledge" is a button that opens `PledgeModal`, the same pop-up the featured candidates and position pages use, because there is no pledge page on the site. Text color adapts to the background variant (white on midnight, dark on cream).

## Image Fallback

If no profile image is provided, a generic person icon placeholder is rendered inside the circular avatar container.

## Accessibility

- Uses semantic `<section>` element
- Main heading uses `heading-lg` or `heading-md` style type depending on name length
- Office title uses `subtitle-1` style type
- Intro uses `body-2`, which ramps from 16px on the phone to 18px from 1280px wide
- The pledge pop-up trigger is a `<button aria-haspopup="dialog">`, not a link
- Image includes alt text derived from the candidate name
