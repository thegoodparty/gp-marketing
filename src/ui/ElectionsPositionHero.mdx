# Elections Position Hero

<Description of={ElectionsPositionHeroStories.FilingOpen} />

## Overview

The hero at the top of every election position page. It names the office and its location,
gives one sentence of context, and carries two dark cards on the right that change with where
the race is in its cycle. There is one component and one Sanity block; the state is read from
the race dates and results by `resolvePositionHeroState` in `src/lib/positionHeroState.ts`,
never chosen by an editor.

## The four states

- **Filing** (`{ phase: 'filing', filingOpen }`): filing deadline, election date, and two countdowns
  ("Days to file", "Days until election"). Before the window opens the first pair reads "Filing
  opens" / "Days until filing opens". Runs from six months before the window opens to the deadline.
- **Mid-election** (`{ phase: 'midElection', resultsPending: false }`): election date, one countdown,
  and a timeline from filing opening to election day with today marked.
- **Results pending** (`{ phase: 'midElection', resultsPending: true }`): election day has passed and
  no result has reached us. The countdown goes, the timeline fills to election day, and the ballot
  card is hidden rather than showing stale "filed so far" copy.
- **Decided** (`{ phase: 'decided', multipleWinners }`): winner card with photo, party, term and pledge
  line; the ballot card becomes "Final results" with a check by each winner. With several winners
  the card adds the other winners' avatars and the button reads "View all N winners".

## Empty states

- `candidates` **undefined** means we hold no candidate data, and the ballot card is not rendered.
  An **empty list** is a genuine zero and renders "0 candidates filed so far".
- A date that is missing simply drops its row and its countdown.
- The decided state renders no winner card until `winners` has an entry.

## Where the data comes from

On the live site the section wrapper (`ElectionsPositionHeroSection`) receives an `OfficeData`
override built by `buildPositionHeroOverride` in `src/lib/electionsTemplateHelpers.tsx`: race dates
and seat count from the race, candidates from `loadPositionHeroCandidates`. Winners and the previous
cycle's winners are not populated yet because election-api records no results; until it does, a race
whose election day has passed shows the results-pending state.
