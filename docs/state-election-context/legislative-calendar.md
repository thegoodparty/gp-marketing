# Legislative calendar and work order

This is the plan for writing the remaining state docs and re-checking finished
ones after each legislative session. It is for planning only. Nothing in it goes
into published copy.

## When each state's legislature meets in 2027

All 50 legislatures hold a regular session in 2027, including the four that meet
only in odd-numbered years (Montana, Nevada, North Dakota and Texas). DC's Council
meets year-round, and Congress can also change DC law.

**Only rows marked "verified" have been read in that state's constitution or
statutes.** The rest are planning estimates based on each state's usual pattern.
They get verified, and the doc gets its exact convening date, when the state's
doc is written.

Re-check a doc about a month after the session ends, once the governor has acted.
If a legislature meets year-round, re-check in January and July.

## Re-check plan for 2027

Grouped by month. Within a month, do the states with the most pages first.

- **January 2027**: the year-round legislatures (Pennsylvania, Illinois, Ohio,
  Michigan, New York, Wisconsin, New Jersey, Massachusetts and DC), to catch
  anything passed in late 2026.
- **April**: Kentucky, New Mexico, South Dakota, Utah, Wyoming.
- **May**: Indiana, Arkansas, Georgia, Tennessee, Idaho, Maryland, Mississippi,
  Virginia, Washington, West Virginia.
- **June**: Illinois (spring session), Minnesota, Iowa, Kansas, Oklahoma, Alaska, Colorado, Florida, Hawaii, Montana, North Dakota,
  South Carolina, Vermont.
- **July**: Pennsylvania, Ohio, Michigan, Texas, New York, Wisconsin, Missouri,
  Nebraska, Louisiana, Oregon, Alabama, Arizona, Connecticut, Delaware, Maine,
  Nevada, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Rhode Island, Massachusetts, DC.
- **October**: North Carolina.
- **November**: California.

Special sessions are common, and in 2026 several dealt with redistricting.
Re-check a state early if a special session touches elections or local
government.
