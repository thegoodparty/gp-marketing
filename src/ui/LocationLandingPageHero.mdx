# Location Landing Page Hero

<Description of={LocationLandingPageHeroStories.Default} />

## Overview

The hero at the top of a location landing page (state, county, city or district). The page supplies the headline and the body copy; beside them the block can show up to three stat cards, and under them up to two buttons that jump further down the page.

## Usage

Use this component when:
- Creating hero sections for state, county, city or district landing pages
- Summarising a location's elections in a few headline figures
- Giving people a way into the races further down the page

## Props

### Prop Details

#### `headline` (optional)

The whole headline, phrased by the page (for example "Upcoming elections in Cook County, Illinois"). Without it the block falls back to the bare location name built from the props below.

**Type:** `string`

#### `locationLevel` (required)

The level of location being displayed. Determines the fallback headline format.

**Type:** `'state' | 'county' | 'city' | 'district'`

#### `stateName` (required)

The state name, used by the fallback headline.

**Type:** `string`

#### `countyName` / `cityName` (optional)

Used by the fallback headline at county, district and city level.

**Type:** `string`

#### `bodyCopy` (optional)

Body copy under the headline. If not provided, defaults to text based on location level.

**Type:** `ReactNode`

#### `stats` (optional)

Up to three stat cards, rendered beside the headline on large screens and stacked under the copy on small ones. Uses the shared `Stat` card in its compact size. When empty, the hero renders as a single column.

**Type:** `StatProps[]`

#### `buttons` (optional)

Up to two buttons under the body copy, usually anchors to sections further down the page. They pick up the outline style that suits the background, and a down arrow, unless the caller overrides either.

**Type:** `ComponentButtonProps[]`

#### `backgroundColor` (optional)

**Type:** `'cream' | 'midnight'` — **Default:** `'cream'`

#### `textAlign` (optional)

**Type:** `'left' | 'center' | 'right'` — **Default:** `'left'`

## Stat Cards

- Use the shared `Stat` card in its compact size, so colors match the Stats Block
- Sit three across beside the headline on large screens, and stack full width below the copy on small ones
- Count up from zero when the value starts with a number; a value that is not a number, such as a placeholder or a date, renders as written
- Drop one type size when the value is long, so a spelled-out election date fits the card the same way a short count does

## On location pages

The section wrapper, not this component, applies three data rules. The figures describe the same ballot the offices list below shows (the place's own races plus its county's and state's), in the year that list opens on:

- The halo green card is the races card. Its number is the count of rows the offices list shows for that year, a real zero included.
- The lavender card is the independents card. Its number is the count of pledged candidates in those races, a real zero included, and the card is left out when that count cannot be trusted (the ballot had more upcoming races than the fetch covers, or the place could not be found). The editor's label stays.
- A button anchored to `#independents` (the featured candidates block's default anchor) is left out when the location has no pledged candidate or officeholder.

## Background Variants

**Midnight (default):** dark background, white copy, white outline buttons.

**Cream:** light background, dark copy, dark outline buttons.
